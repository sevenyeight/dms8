Entry 01: The Name

![Seveny Eight](/assets/images/blog_images/78profile.jpeg)

Seveny Eight is 1978.  

The year I arrived for my stay on this planet.  

I'd always felt something in the rhythm of it — seventy-eight — the way it moves when you say it out loud.  

But somewhere between thinking and typing, the "t" dropped.


Seveny.


Not a typo.  

A discovery.  

That's how we actually say it, all in one breath, one word, the "t" swallowed before it ever gets out.


It became its own thing in that moment.  
A little slang.  

A little mine.  

It gave me distance and room to become something else — 
something that likes to make weird looking creatures, and robots
as well as a love for nature, 
and can't stop thinking about what happens when nature and technology clash.  

Not peacefully.  

Not easily.  

They exist at the edge of each other, always at the precipice.  

If one falls, the other takes over.  
Machine over nature.  

Nature over machine.  

The tension is the point.  


Seveny Eight isn't an artist.  

Artists carry a weight of classification  
— style, medium, school of thought.  

Seveny Eight is a creator.  
The medium is whatever the creation calls for.  

The objective is the thing being made.  
The joy is in the making of it.


78
