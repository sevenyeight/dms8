Attempted Humans.  
That’s what I envision with these weird things I create.  

![Chicken Leg Person](/assets/images/blog_images/clp001.jpg)

I imagine them in motion.  
Morphing painfully until at the end it’s human looking, or at least a close attempt.  

They are captured in the process of becoming, and in this case, this thing looks like a hungry chicken leg.  

Process wise, I start this with a random line
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp000.png)  

From there I’ll make more of a mess, getting rid of all that distracting white space.  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp0001.png)  

Once that is to my satisfaction, I’ll switch to the Smudge Tool, which I will use for about 90% of this piece.  
The objective here is to remove all those lines and work out the general shape of the “figure”.  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp0002.png)  

I’ll add a few more details here, like this mouth and tongue.  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp0003.jpg)  

From here the fun begins.  
Using the Smudge Tool, I’ll dive into details.  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp002.jpg)  

I’ll spend hours working on details  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp003.jpg)  

Pushing and pulling pixels around is actually very relaxing  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp004.jpg)  

Giving this thing some form of LIFE. A painful one by the looks of it
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp0004.jpg)  

At the end, after many hours and days, it looks like this  
![Chicken Leg Person Detail](/assets/images/blog_images/clp001.jpg)  

Stay tuned for next time when we add color to this thing.
